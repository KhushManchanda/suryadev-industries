Whole holding

The triangle is held to its register area. Sides 11 / 47 / 49 as chained give only 257.9 kar² by Heron — 30.6 gaj short of the paper figure — so the Reg gaj box (897.42) drives it instead: the 47 and 49 stay put and the base opens to 11.38, which is what those three chained lengths would have to be to hold that area. Clear the box to go back to pure Heron. The 3-karam strip on the west and south is drawn as a metalled side road and back road, and left out of the area.

1 gaj = 1 square yard = 9 sq ft. Marla / kanal / acre converted from square feet at 272.25 sq ft per marla. Road width is illustrative, not measured.
